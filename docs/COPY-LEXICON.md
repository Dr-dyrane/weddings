# Guest Copy Lexicon

Guest language is ceremonial, direct, and human. Internal architecture vocabulary never becomes product copy.

## Approved language

- Open your invitation
- View invitation details
- Reserved for {guest or household}
- Together with their families
- The wedding circle
- Celebration details
- Add to calendar
- Get directions
- Share invitation
- Kindly reply
- Your place is reserved
- Edit response
- Created with Dyrane Weddings

## Banned in guest UI

`world`, `scene`, `engine`, `manifest`, `render`, `WebGL`, `3D mode`, `quality tier`, `token`, `OG`, `simulation`, `model`, `AI`, `pipeline`, `generated`, and `loading assets`.

The words may appear in internal documentation, Studio diagnostics, or source code. They must not appear in invitation metadata, share cards, visible guest copy, accessible names, errors, URLs, or analytics event labels.

## Tone rules

- Prefer a specific noun over a product metaphor: invitation, story, celebration, details, reply.
- Use familiar action labels. Poetry may introduce a chapter but must not replace a task label.
- Errors state what happened, what was preserved, and the next available action.
- Do not claim an RSVP, email, share, or calendar action succeeded until its responsible system confirms it.
