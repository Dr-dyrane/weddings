# Alexander and Chioma Yardstick Art Bible

Status: locked for blockout; real details remain provisional

Direction: **Twilight Garden → Glass Pavilion**

Working title: **The Living Ceremony**

## North star

Opening the invitation should feel like stepping out of ordinary life, walking through a twilight garden and entering a luminous wedding pavilion prepared specifically for Alexander, Chioma and the named guest.

The experience is not futuristic because it contains chrome or neon. It is futuristic because the environment responds with impossible precision while remaining emotionally believable.

## Emotional sequence

1. **Anticipation:** a quiet, formal envelope waits in negative space.
2. **Recognition:** the recipient sees that the invitation is theirs.
3. **Threshold:** the seal releases and the card becomes an opening.
4. **Wonder:** a garden and ribbon of light extend beyond the card.
5. **Intimacy:** objects and framed memories reveal the couple's story.
6. **Belonging:** family, wedding party and vendors occupy the wedding circle.
7. **Arrival:** the guest reaches the glass pavilion and sees the date and place.
8. **Participation:** RSVP feels like taking one's place, not completing admin.
9. **Anticipation renewed:** the pavilion remains glowing after the reply.

## World grammar

### Exterior — twilight garden

- Deep plum sky moving toward indigo, never pure black.
- Sculpted foliage, white flowers and restrained lilac blooms.
- Warm pin lights and candle points establish depth.
- A pale stone or pearl-silk path bends rather than runs straight at the camera.
- Distant pavilion glow provides orientation from the beginning.
- Air contains sparse pollen, haze and petals; never constant confetti.

### Interior — luminous glass pavilion

- Clear and smoked glass, warm brass, pearl textile and dark timber.
- Open doors and transparent walls preserve the garden connection.
- Reflective surfaces are broad and soft, not mirror-chrome product renders.
- Tables, flowers and fabric suggest guests are expected without rendering a crowd.
- The RSVP surface belongs to the architecture: a place card, guest book or illuminated seating card.

### Ceremonial paper

- Heavy warm ivory with a subtle fibre texture.
- Black typographic ink and burnished champagne-gold accents.
- Gilded edge expressed as a narrow travelling highlight.
- The envelope seal carries only the couple’s slanted `A & C` lockup.
  Do not add a heart, generic infinity or stock wedding symbol.

## Material hierarchy

1. Paper fibre and ink establish formality.
2. Glass creates the transition between outside and inside.
3. Silk and organza soften geometry.
4. Burnished metal catches intentional highlights.
5. Foliage and flowers make the world alive.
6. Haze and particles create depth only where light reveals them.

Avoid liquid chrome, holographic gradients, generic purple neon, excessive bloom, plastic florals, glitter shaders and stock luxury emblems.

The couple-specific monogram may appear as a subtle foil or emboss. Use the same
frame-free italic initials at every size; never add a wreath, shield or border,
and never composite the old raster reference as a finished logo.

## Light direction

- Key light travels from soft dusk to pavilion warmth across the journey.
- The recipient name and couple names receive the clearest contrast.
- Reflection changes are limited to pointer or device movement within a few degrees.
- Bloom is reserved for distant lamps, the seal break and the pavilion arrival.
- Every scene has a dark anchoring region for readable HTML copy.

## Camera language

- Human eye height; no drone spectacle during intimate moments.
- Slow push, gentle lateral reveal and architectural passage are preferred.
- One major camera event per chapter.
- No barrel roll, orbit, snap zoom, infinite tunnel or uncontrolled depth dive.
- Forward scroll advances; reverse scroll restores the earlier composition faithfully.
- Mobile uses shorter travel, fewer foreground occluders and larger subject scale.

## Typography

- Display serif: ceremonial names, chapter titles and large date moments.
- Quiet sans: directions, RSVP, accessibility and utilities.
- Script or calligraphic face: one short accent only after the final brand direction is approved.
- Copy never sits directly on visually noisy foliage or specular highlights.
- Type remains DOM text. Texture and light may frame it but never rasterize essential information.

## Sound direction

Default: silent.

Optional soundscape after explicit guest intent:

- distant evening garden tone;
- soft fabric and paper movement at the opening;
- a restrained glass or bell harmonic at the threshold;
- distant instrumental warmth near the pavilion;
- no lyrics, narration or looping melody that competes with reading.

Sound fades when the document is hidden and can be muted instantly from every chapter.

## Cultural specificity

The experience should feel Nigerian through generosity, scale, warmth, textile intelligence and the importance of family—not through generic “African” motifs.

Potential Igbo or family-specific geometry, language, coral, lace and ceremonial references remain open until Alexander and Chioma approve them. Do not invent sacred symbols, titles, fabric meanings or family crests.

## Image-generation direction

Generated concept art may establish composition and lighting, but final media records must retain prompt, model, date, rights, intended chapter and approval state.

Base prompt vocabulary:

> Cinematic twilight wedding garden leading into a contemporary glass pavilion, warm ivory and champagne light, restrained lilac flowers, deep plum sky, Nigerian luxury expressed through textile richness and hospitality rather than clichés, editorial realism, believable architecture, generous negative space, soft atmospheric depth, no people, no text, no crowns, no neon, no fantasy castle.

Generate each chapter as separate desktop and portrait-mobile compositions. Do not crop desktop concepts into mobile after approval.

## Definition of “state of the art”

The yardstick passes only when:

- the first frame is already beautiful before WebGL loads;
- the threshold transition produces a genuine change of place;
- every chapter has a recognisable composition at rest;
- mobile feels authored rather than reduced;
- details and RSVP remain effortless;
- reduced motion still feels like a premium editorial invitation;
- the environment stays responsive on reference phones;
- no effect can be described as a technology demo.
