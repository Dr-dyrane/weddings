## Separate product lanes

- `EVENT-COLLABORATION-LANE.md` — future people, roles, vendor credits and
  QR-accessible guest-media collection. It is deliberately outside the current
  six-chapter invitation and carries its own access, consent and moderation
  decisions.
- `EVENT-COLLABORATION-OPERATIONS.md` — activation, event-day operation,
  privacy, recovery and production-owner requirements for that lane.

## Status language

- **Locked:** approved direction; implementation may proceed without reopening it.
- **Provisional:** safe for simulation and blockout; must be approved before a real publication.
- **Open:** missing decision or asset; use the named fallback and do not invent a final answer.
